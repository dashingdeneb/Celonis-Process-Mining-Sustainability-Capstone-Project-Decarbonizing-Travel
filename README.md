**Road** **to** **Net** **Zero** **by** **2030**

We have committed to an ambitious goal to lower our carbon emissions to
be Net Zero by

2030\. Our internal analysis indicates that the bulk of our Scope 3
carbon emissions stems

from employee business travel.

**What** **are** **Scope** **3** **Emissions?**Scope 3 emissions are
indirect greenhouse gas emissions that

occur in a company's value chain. Unlike Scope 1 (direct emissions from
owned sources) or

Scope 2 (emissions from purchased electricity), Scope 3 includes
activities from assets not

owned or controlled by us, such as the flights, trains, and hotels our
employees use for

business travel.

Currently, our travel booking process is decentralized. While this has
historically prioritized

cost savings and employee convenience, we now see an opportunity to
integrate carbon

efficiency into the heart of our travel operations. We are looking to
evolve our processes by:



> ● **Integrating** **Carbon** **Metrics:** Incorporating emission data
> directly into the booking and
>
> approval workflow.
>
> ● **Enhancing** **Automation:** Increasing the automation rate for
> policy checks to ensure
>
> sustainable choices are made consistently.
>
> ● **Driving** **Transparency:** Creating a clear system of carbon
> accounting to track,
>
> measure, and report on our performance.

We believe Celonis is the best tool to identify a dynamic strategy for
efficient decarbonization

in a high-growth business environment. Our goal is to prioritize the
reduction of

high-emission travel while maintaining the agility of our global teams.
